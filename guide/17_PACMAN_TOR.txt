## 17_PACMAN_TOR

## Targeted attacks can tamper with downloads between you and the mirror. Routing pacman through tor removes that vector.
## Edit and uncomment the following lines in pacman.conf:
sudo vim /etc/pacman.conf
```ini
XferCommand = /usr/bin/curl --socks5-hostname localhost:9050 --location --continue-at - --fail --output %o %u
```

## Tor must be started for pacman to reach any mirror, since tor is not started at boot.
