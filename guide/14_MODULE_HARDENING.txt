## Disable Netfilter's automatic connection tracking helper assignment. Open the file:
sudo vim /etc/modprobe.d/30-conntrack.conf
```ini
## Conntrack:
## Disables the automatic assignment of connection tracking helpers in Netfilter.
## It drags extra functionality such as IRC protocol parsing into the kernel.
## Switching it off shrinks the kernel attack surface.
options nf_conntrack nf_conntrack_helper=0
```
